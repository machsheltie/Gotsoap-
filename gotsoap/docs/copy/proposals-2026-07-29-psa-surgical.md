## Unit 3 — Unholy: CLEAR

Deleting `The water is right there.` removes the clipped conversion button without damaging the
sermon form. The collection-plate question now owns the landing, and no second sentence arrives to
translate it into a command.

R1 calls the reader “cornered”; the copy does not perform that mechanism, and it does not need to.
The congregation's unanswered question is the close. That is still a distinct interrogative move,
and the page's pull and Sniff Test band retain the conversion path outside the case note.

**Ruling:** clear as written.

## Unit 4 — Redemption: CHALLENGE, one explanatory clause

The full reframe solves the twinning defect. Unholy keeps the pew, blessing, and collection plate;
Redemption now runs on a return/exchange transaction, and the friends' disbelief is a genuine
anecdotal close rather than an imperative button. `They don't believe it was just soap.` survives
the challenge.

One opening clause announces a metaphor the rest of the paragraph already performs:

> "The redemption on offer is the retail kind:"

It also names the wrong transaction with unnecessary precision. In retail, a coupon, voucher, or
points are redeemed; this man trades in a can, takes a refund, and completes an exchange. The
self-audit says the trade/refund language decodes the retail sense before `redemption` must resolve,
but `redemption` appears first and labels the device for the reader. That is the same
trust-the-reader cost removed from `That's the entire product.` in the prior case-note round.

Delete that clause only and begin:

> "He traded the can in and took the refund in water. One shower — the cheapest exchange on
> record — and every room he'd ever emptied reopened for business. His friends keep asking
> what changed. They don't believe it was just soap."

The page title still supplies *Redemption*; the case note answers it with a deliberate retail
refusal of the church instead of explaining the alternate definition.

**Ruling:** narrow challenge. The new metaphor and anecdotal landing clear; the taxonomy sentence
does not.

## Unit 5 — Public Thirst Announcement: CHALLENGE, predicate repair

The structural move is right. The double imperative is gone, the maintenance beat appears once,
and a validity condition is native to a public announcement.

The final comparison does not quite say the intended condition:

> "This announcement remains in effect for exactly as long as the showers do."

Grammatically, `do` inherits `remain in effect`; semantically, an individual shower lasts only
until the water stops. The line can therefore read as an announcement that expires when the shower
ends, not a status maintained by continued showering. The ambiguity weakens the exact causality the
new closer exists to carry.

Recommended repair:

> "The background check was your mother, and he passed. The dinner reservation stands. One
> shower put him in the amber light. This announcement remains in effect for exactly as long
> as he keeps showering."

That keeps the officialese, validity condition, and zero-imperative design while making the
maintenance requirement explicit.

**Ruling:** narrow challenge to `the showers do`; the closer species clears.
