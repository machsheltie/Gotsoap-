# Got Soap? behavioral world bible

## Core belief

Got Soap? changes behavior through erotic aspiration, not health education, humiliation, or shame.
The man is a potential convert, not an enemy. The woman is a witness and recruiter, not the operator
of a grievance forum. Cleanliness is the price of admission to the man he already imagines himself
becoming.

## How behavior changes

The campaign makes cleanliness feel like the price of admission to a more magnetic version of the self. It recruits through desire, theatrical certainty, and a joke delivered with enough conviction to make participation feel inevitable. It never corrects, scolds, diagnoses, or converts the visitor into a health-education audience.

## The man

The man is a potential convert, not an enemy. Cleanliness is the price of admission to the man he already imagines himself becoming.

## The woman

The woman is a witness and recruiter, not the operator of a grievance forum. She receives language worth sharing and an invitation to recruit, never a demand to administer, prove, or carry the campaign's grievance.

## What Got Soap? finds funny

Got Soap? finds funny the overcommitted seriousness of presenting soap as a cinematic threshold: a tiny act of hygiene staged with fragrance-ad drama, civic conviction, and unreasonable glamour. The laugh comes from the scale of the promise and the campaign's refusal to wink out of it.

## What it never jokes about

The campaign never jokes by humiliating men, treating women as a grievance bloc, or turning cleanliness into a moral worth test. It does not borrow CWAAA's advocacy labor or the Office's procedural certainty as a punchline.

## Desire and cleanliness vocabulary

Use desire, aspiration, conversion, admission, movement, confidence, smolder, lather, steam, proof, and clean-man energy. Keep cleanliness concrete and sensory rather than clinical. Avoid diagnosis, compliance, pathology, shame, correction, and public-health framing.

## Utility voice

Utility copy remains campaign-authored: loading sustains anticipation without performing a startup status update; validation is direct, private, and useful; errors tell the visitor what failed and offer a real retry; confirmation gives the campaign its committed payoff. Shop language presents products as campaign performance, even when checkout is unavailable. Download language treats a poster as campaign material; share language recruits a witness; film framing announces a premiere rather than a generic video asset.

The utility voice never uses startup jargon, public-health lectures, generic empowerment copy, or premature Hope2 Studio explanation.

## What Got Soap? knows

Got Soap? knows its invitation, posters, broadcast, Shop performance, Sniff Test, campaign pledge edition, and campaign artifacts. It may point to CWAAA at a designed seam, but it does not explain CWAAA's internal work, the Office, their relationship, or protected shared unknowns.

## What breaks the illusion

The illusion breaks when the campaign becomes a product grid, a generic video card, an operational storefront, a public-health lecture, a grievance forum, or an early portfolio explanation. It also breaks when campaign utility copy sounds like a startup dashboard or tries to resolve the wider institutional world.

## Page feelings

Home turns intrigue into recruitment. Poster routes turn attention into devotion and shareability. The Sniff Test turns recognition into a named campaign identity. The pledge turns declaration into a social act. Shop turns browsing into a staged product performance. Broadcast turns viewing into attendance at a campaign premiere. About waits until the fiction has paid off before revealing authorship.

## Interaction boundaries

Interactions may heighten invitation, reveal, sharing, and declaration; they may not humiliate, score a person's worth, simulate a health intervention, or make an unavailable checkout look operational. The Sniff Test remains Got Soap? field theatre with no numeric score. The campaign edition of the pledge follows the shared contract, while CWAAA receives the informational seam only where configured.
