### OLC-L01 — Office pen

The pen is ordinary institutional swag whose 1961 continuity may become meaningful through recurrence. It must not announce itself as a clue, explain what happened in 1961, or become a collectible puzzle key.

**Before production:** approve the exact complete imprint, whether/how the established date appears, pen model/material, imprint method, number of colors, supplier's actual imprint area, quantity and event use. The website typography law does not by itself define a physical-object identity system; read the artifact/world authority and get a concrete approval.

**Deliverables after approval:** proposed `office-pen-imprint-v1.svg` plus supplier-required vector PDF and a review proof. Dimensions, bleed and color specification must come from the chosen object's manufacturing template. Keep an editable text version and a production outlined version where the supplier requires it; preserve font/license provenance. Do not guess a millimeter measurement now.

**Acceptance:** readable ordinary imprint, plausible manufacturing, approved wording/continuity, verified print rights, no explanation of jurisdiction or operational relationship. There is no associated Office shop page.

### OLC-L02 — Office business card or event-table artifact

Create only after its real artifact function and complete copy are approved. It may recur on the owner's event table, but co-location with campaign or coalition items never proves institutional control or affiliation.

**Interview:** exact front/back content, role of any contact line, whether that contact is real and authorized, dimensions, paper/finish, print method, quantities, date/version and recurrence context. Do not reuse `1-800-GOT-SOAP` as an ordinary Office contact number or invent a working agency service.

**Deliverables after approval:** proposed `office-card-v1-front.pdf`, `office-card-v1-back.pdf` only if a reverse is approved, plus editable source and proof. Bleed/safe zones and vector/raster requirements follow the chosen printer. No web dimensions or crop derivatives are currently required.

**Acceptance:** complete plausible object, approved contact implications, ordinary institutional presentation and protected unknowns intact; no website badge or navigation created from it.
