## Image law — settled, not open for discussion

**No still life anywhere on this site. People are shown — faces, bodies, the actual members and the
actual men.** Never substitute an object, texture, empty room, hand-only crop, back-of-head, or
silhouette for the person a section is about, and never blur, obscure, or crop out a face to soften
it. The home hero is the posed coalition group portrait (CW-G02, shipped); the recovery voice is
Brayden's own participant photograph (CW-G04, shipped). Both are closed.

Every person in this fiction is **AI-generated**. There is no real likeness, no consent question, and
no subject who can be hurt or embarrassed. Do not raise likeness, dignity, privacy, or possible
offense as a reason to soften, abstract, or object-ify an image — those arguments do not apply to
synthetic people, and the owner has ruled on this directly. This is satire; being uncomfortable is
part of its job, and hedging is what makes it fall flat.

What stays rejected is the **generic and institutional**, never the human: stock testimonial
carousels, charity-hero framing, matching-shirt volunteer filler, clipboard enthusiasm, mugshot
grids, surveillance framing, humiliation, and before/after construction.

An object delivered where a person belongs is a rejected asset. Full text: `GRAPHICS-TO-MAKE.md`
§ Standing image law and `design.md` §7. **Earlier documents that refuse posed faces or prefer
"materially specific still lifes" are struck. Do not restore, re-derive, or re-argue them.**
