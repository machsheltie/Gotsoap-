# Reader evidence — RC-073, Derek, read back to back with RC-064, Billy Bob

**2026-09-28.** Three blind reads (Maya, Dylan, Priya), each given a specific reading order: Billy
Bob's record first, in full, then Derek's immediately after — the way a visitor experiences it by
clicking Billy Bob's restored "Next record" link. No brief, no canon docs, no framing beyond the
persona and the two records (Brayden's record was offered as optional context for "an ordinary
record" on the site). Readers were asked their normal felt-experience questions, then a direct
question the owner asked for: **read back to back, does Derek feel like a fresh case or a formulaic
reskin of Billy Bob, and where exactly does the resemblance sit?**

## 1 · Verdict, all three readers

**Convergent: the skeleton repeats; the content does not.** All three independently located the
resemblance in the same two or three specific beats, not in the record as a whole, and all three
separately praised the same specific divergences. This is close to fact, not a single reader's taste.

## 2 · Where the resemblance sits (named by all three)

- **The caseworker's closing line.** Billy Bob: *"I have nothing left to offer him that he has not
  already heard and declined."* Derek: *"I have no additional voluntary intervention to offer."*
  Same clause order, same rhythm, same position in the document, same rhetorical move (state the
  limit, imply moral clarity, exit). Priya: "read once, it's a device... read twice in ninety
  seconds, it's a macro." Maya: "the moment I felt most like I was reading a mail-merge." Dylan
  independently flagged the same pair of lines without prompting on the specific wording.
- **The chronology's repeated-refusal device.** Billy Bob repeats *"Never saw much point in washing
  just to get dirty again"* three times verbatim as the man never budges. Derek repeats *"I'm still
  getting dates"* the same way. All three clocked this as the identical mechanism run twice. Maya:
  "the first time it landed as 'oh, that's how they show he never budges.' The second time it landed
  as 'oh, that's the house style.'" Priya: "clever in Billy Bob... in Derek it reads as the writer
  reaching for the same lever because it worked last time."
- **The Office finding block.** Same field template, same caps-lock voice, "PARTICIPANT
  COMPREHENSION: NOT AT ISSUE" verbatim in both. Two readers (Maya, Priya) explicitly separated this
  from the two flags above: because it is diegetically a government form, the repetition can read as
  intentional (bureaucracy re-using its own template) rather than lazy — but both warned a third
  record repeating it unchanged would break that read. Maya: "if a third one in a row used the exact
  same... sign-off, I'd start rolling my eyes at the format itself rather than at the man."
- **The intake form's shape.** Same field labels in the same order, same "Prior contact with CWAAA:
  None" placement, same rhetorical slot for the participant's flattest self-justifying quote as his
  opening "position." Named by Maya and Priya as the least Derek-specific part of the record.

## 3 · Where Derek earns its own read (also named by all three)

- **The corroboration shape is a different mechanism, not a reskin.** Billy Bob has one witness (the
  spouse); Derek has four independent reporting parties who did not know each other, with
  cross-referral corroboration confirmed three separate times. Maya: "that's not swapped-in, that's
  a different guy" — it produces a genuinely new feeling (validation-by-numbers) Billy Bob's file has
  no room for.
- **The specific imagery is Derek's own and rated stronger than Billy Bob's by two readers.** The
  soap-on-a-rope "hanging in the shower like it had been put there as a joke," the folded, unused
  towels in the closet, the fabric refresher on the toilet. Priya: "better than anything in Billy
  Bob's intake." Maya: "genuinely great, specific, funny-horrible image."
- **The ending is the strongest, most-praised divergence.** Billy Bob resolves warm: reconciliation,
  a photograph, "put down that it's been resolved." Derek resolves cold: blocked calls, unclaimed
  certified mail, a blank disposition, no follow-up, no photograph, no Next. All three called this
  out unprompted as the record's real point of difference. Priya: "Derek unresolved is the site
  earning 'archive' instead of 'case study binder.'" Dylan (the persona least primed to notice craft)
  still felt it: "no forgiveness... it lands as bleaker, more clipped, like the file just got closed
  instead of closed with a bow."
- **Derek's own rationalization is a different register.** Billy Bob is fatalism ("no point washing,
  I'll be dirty again"); Derek is optimism-as-defense, externally validated ("I still get dates,
  I don't see where the emergency is") — Dylan, the reader closest to Derek's demographic,
  specifically recognized his own logic in this register ("I don't use deodorant because I'm already
  using body spray... that's my exact reasoning") in a way he did not recognize anything in Billy
  Bob's.

## 4 · What this means for record 8

The one specific warning repeated by two readers: this device (caseworker sign-off macro, chronology
refrain) survives being run twice in a row on the strength of Derek's own material. It will not
survive a third unchanged repetition in record 8. Whoever writes Carlos Eduardo should treat the
caseworker's closing line and the chronology's repeated-line device as **spent** — reuse the
underlying idea (an institutional voice running out of patience; a participant's fixed line recited
back at him) but not the same sentence shape or rhetorical beat, or the pattern itself becomes the
joke instead of the man.

## 5 · Shareability (Maya)

Maya would send Derek to a group chat ("the soap-on-a-rope like it was a JOKE... I HAVE MET THIS
MAN"), not Billy Bob, whose record she'd send privately to one friend in that situation, not for a
laugh. Derek is the more shareable record of the two.
