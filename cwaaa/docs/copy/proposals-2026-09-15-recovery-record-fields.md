# Proposal — Recovery Record field vocabulary (CWAAA register)

**Vivian, 2026-09-15. Dials: Spice 7 / Darkness 3 / Wrongness 1.** Brayden's file is the format
lesson. It is deliberately the least strange document on the route, because the embedded Office
fragment in the severe case has to arrive somewhere. Spending wrongness here costs it there.

**One true thing:** a woman filled out a form about a man who has never filled out a form in his
life.

**Costume:** volunteer intake paperwork. Law 3 — the joke is the seriousness of the baseline, never
the participant.

**The canon problem and the fix.** `Audit Type`, `Disposition`, and anything that concludes belong
to the Office. But the comedic engine inside `Pre-Date Compliance` is not the word *compliance* — it
is that **there is a date on the calendar and a deadline attached to a man's body.** That survives
intact in CWAAA's verbs. Nothing funny is lost; it changes letterhead and gets funnier, because a
nonprofit noting a scheduled meeting is more absurd than an agency ordering a wash.

Second fix, unasked: the owner's block reads as a dating-profile referral, and RC-022's approved
quote credits a roommate with a printout. Both are true and the file is better for it. The
prospective match files the referral. The roommate's printout arrives as supporting material from a
different direction. Two unrelated people looked at this man in the same week and independently
reached for paperwork.

## 1 · The referral block — labels, and Brayden's values as set

> **REFERRAL AS RECEIVED**
>
> Participant — Brayden, 27
> Reporting party — Prospective match
> Referral occasioned by — First date hygiene audit
> Participant's stated position — "King looking for his Queen to spoil."
> Stated expectations of others — "Must be fit, fun, 10/10, no drama."
> Primary self-representation — Fish
> Reported shower substitute — Febreze
> Water engagement — Reported infrequent
> Supporting materials — Printout, submitted separately by a member of the household
> Prior contact with CWAAA — None

Every label is a verb CWAAA owns: it receives, records, and describes. Not one of them concludes
anything. `Primary self-representation — Fish` is the load-bearing line and it must stay one word
with no article. The moment it becomes "a photograph of a fish" it is a caption, and this particular
field loses its comic compression. CWAAA is still allowed a position elsewhere.

## 2 · The closing outcome line

> **Intervention provided** — Baseline materials
> **Follow-up interval** — 90 days
> **Current status** — Maintaining baseline
> **Outcome** — Lathers unprompted
> **Case status** — Closed

**Outcome: Lathers unprompted.**

It is the owner's sketch with the padding cut and one word doing the work the other four were
splitting. It also steals the Office's own construction — `Disposition: Lather required` — and
inverts the power inside it. Same two words. The Office orders. CWAAA observes. A reader who meets
the fragment in the severe case later will feel that rhyme without being able to name it, which is
the entire reason to build the pattern here first.

## 3 · The generalizing rule, for the other five

**For Brayden's referral block, exactly one field carries the participant's own words, rendered flat
and unremarked.** The fish is where this block's comedy lives; every other field is straight
procedure holding it upright. Two quoted fields compete for that one comic beat. This is a timing
decision for RC-022, not a general rule that CWAAA cannot make judgments or show controlled affect.

**In the outcome line, the load sits on one adverb that implies the prior state without describing
it.** *Unprompted* convicts every previous shower of having been somebody's idea. Marcus, Gary,
Chad, and Kaelthas each get their own adverb and it is never *unprompted* again — the adverb is the
participant's signature, and reusing it would flatten six men into one joke.

## Self-audit

1. One true thing — yes, stated above. 2. Punching up — every field describes a choice; none
describes a body. 3. Apology sweep — no hedges; nothing softens the fish. 4. First read — the block
reads as real intake at a glance and lands on the second pass through. 5. Cut test — `Outcome` went
from four words to two and hit harder; `Primary self-representation` earns its length by being the
one grandiose label in the set. 6. Last line — `Case status — Closed` is the button, and it is flat
on purpose: this man required no further resources. 7. Specificity — Febreze, 90 days, 27, 10/10,
all verbatim or exact. 8. AI-tell sweep — no tidy triads, no throat-clearing, no line asking
permission. 9. Dials — Wrongness held at 1 deliberately. 10. References — none forced; the only
borrowed engine is the Office's own two-word construction, unlabeled and unexplained.

# Addendum, 2026-09-15 — RC-022 testimony and referral statements

**One flag on ratification.** `First date hygiene audit` uses **audit**, which canon assigns to the
Office. Proposed: `First date hygiene inquiry`. CWAAA inquires. It does not audit. Everything else
in the approved block stands as edited.

**Where these land.** The two referral statements sit with the referral block, set as institutional
captions in Catesque. Brayden's paragraph is the record's testimony field in Modest. The existing
approved RC-022 line stays the index pull-quote — the paragraph opens by stepping past it rather
than repeating it, so the two never sit in a reader's ear as the same sentence twice.

## Referral statements

> **REPORTING PARTY — STATEMENT ON FILE**
>
> "His profile said he was looking for a queen to spoil. Every photo was him holding a fish, and in
> one of them I could see his sheets. I wasn't going to cancel. I was going to go, and then handle
> it myself. Then I found out there was somewhere to send it. I didn't cancel. I filed."

> **MEMBER OF HOUSEHOLD — STATEMENT ON FILE**
>
> "I'd said it out loud twice and nothing happened. So I printed the page and put it on his door. I
> didn't want a confrontation. I wanted a document."

She ends on *I filed.* He ends on *a document.* That echo is deliberate and is the file's whole
thesis: two people who had never spoken to each other looked at this man in the same week and
independently reached for paperwork. It is also the reason the referral block is funny before a
single field is read.

Neither is named. CWAAA protects participants and reporting parties alike, and the relationship
labels carry everything the reader needs.

## How she knew — the corroboration structure

He tells on himself. She is not conducting hygiene surveillance on dating apps; he volunteers it in
pre-date messaging, thinking he is being charmingly self-deprecating, something close to *"don't
judge me, I haven't done laundry all week, Febreze is carrying the team."* She has recently
encountered CWAAA. Referral submitted. The roommate's arrives independently.

That produces the block's best institutional beat — corroboration, which is what actually justifies
a file:

> **REFERRAL 1**
> Source relationship — Cohabitating roommate
> Concern — Repeated fragrance substitution
>
> **REFERRAL 2 — RECEIVED 36 HOURS LATER**
> Source relationship — Prospective romantic partner
> Prior contact with participant — Digital only
> Concern — Repeated fragrance substitution
> Cross-referral corroboration — Confirmed

`Prior contact with participant — Digital only` is the line that earns the laugh: she filed on a man
she has never been in a room with, and she was right.

The two referral statements above stand. The reporting party's closer becomes *"I didn't cancel. I
filed."* — she still goes on the date, which matters now that we know the date went well.
