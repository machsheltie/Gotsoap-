# 2 · Editorial rationale

**Take.** He understood her completely and refused anyway, and the thing his efficiency actually
eliminated was his wife's desire for him.

**Engine — one string that does not move while the institution around it escalates.** The chronology
quotes his position three times and the value is *identical each time*, to the letter, while the
labels above and below it change from outreach to intervention to exhaustion. Rotating his phrasing
would have been variation; a reader scanning a fielded chronology sees a repeated cell and
understands stasis faster than any sentence could say it. It also keeps the chronology from becoming
a second narrative — it is a table with one stuck row.

**The causal spine, and why it drives three separate blocks.** The owner's decision that his wife is
not responsible through failure to explain isn't only a thing to avoid writing; it is the record's
strongest available material, so I made proving comprehension the institution's actual job here. It
appears four times in four registers: `Participant's awareness of concern — Reported as explicit and
repeated`; her "he has never once acted like he didn't hear me"; his own "I could tell it back to you
word for word"; and the field note's "I asked him to tell me back what his wife had told him." The
record closes off every sympathetic reading except the true one. His last line — "I listen. I just
don't agree." — is the engine in six words and is where I'd put any pull quote.

**Conversion argument.** Front-loaded deliberately. The three-beat summary sits at the top as the
record's abstract, so a woman scanning gets *His wife stopped wanting him → He started showering
after every shift* before she reads a word of proof. The reward is then delivered as involuntary
movement rather than approval: "I put my hand on him the other night without thinking about it
first… I didn't have to decide to do it." Desire that doesn't require a decision is the exact thing
the before-state destroyed, which makes it the only correct payoff. Then she refuses to describe the
rest — "The rest of it is mine. Put down that it's been resolved." That withholding sells more than a
description would, and it rhymes structurally with the Office's blacked-out disposition without
either speaker noticing.

**The Office, made stranger by understatement.** Two moves only. `PARTICIPANT COMPREHENSION: NOT AT
ISSUE` dismisses in four words the thing CWAAA spent an entire record establishing — a colder
altitude looking down on the file it is embedded in. `PARTICIPANT PRESENT: YES` is an ordinary
attendance field that answers nothing and quietly confirms he went somewhere. Neither explains
anything, and nothing in the visible record reaches into the interval.

**One field earning its keep:** `Fragrance substitution — Not reported`. He is not the coalition's
usual man. The form that catches the other five does not describe him, which is the quietest
available explanation for why ordinary intervention failed — offered as a data absence, never as a
sentence.

**Audit results (memory.md, run against the prose above).** *Fact-authority:* new particulars are
enumerated in §3; every unratified specific is TK or listed as PROPOSAL / DRAFT TEXTURE. *Causal
integrity:* Revision 3's "she gets mad instead of telling me" and "I never got an answer that was an
answer" are struck — they relocated causal responsibility onto her communication, which the owner's
decision forbids and which also weakened the engine. Agency stays in the verbs: heard, understood,
restated, declined. *Rejected-objective persistence:* the rejected function (his refusal explained by
an information gap) is not preserved through substitute facts, structure, or attributed speech; the
replacement objective is full comprehension plus sustained refusal, and it governs selection
throughout. *Deliberate absences:* the interval between `External review requested` and `Case
returned` is unfilled; no line, label, or quote reaches into it. The quote appears once, at ordinary
scale, with nothing built around it. *Ending:* the record's last line is the canon Coalition
sentence, cold after her heat, commenting on nothing.

**Salvaged from Revision 3:** the field note's existence and its placement as the last human voice;
the structural sequence of blocks; TK discipline on dates. Each survives on its own merits here.
Everything else is rewritten from the engine.
