## The diagnosis being answered

1. **The home clears its throat.** The premise ("routine washing is reasonable") is argued three
   times — in `about.statement`, in the APPROVED subline under it, and again inside `dao.run`
   Advocate. "A practical way to act" appears in two consecutive blocks. Priya: "the site asking me
   to agree the premise is fair. Nobody asked." Maya: "padding." **The fix is subtraction.** Only
   the APPROVED subline keeps the premise; every other slot stops arguing it and does something.
2. **DOCUMENT. ADVOCATE. ORGANIZE. reads as filler** because the three clauses restate their own
   verbs. The clauses now carry one concrete operational fact each and nothing else. They are also
   written to *differ in length* — the run renders as three equal columns, and unequal copy is the
   only lever available against the "three equal cards" read.
3. **Calibration, per all three readers:** specific, dumb, physical, procedural. The elevator
   methodology note and "Some are load-bearing" are the target. Rejected: tidy aphorism, balanced
   antithesis, two-word rimshots, any line that narrates its own moral.

## 1 · `home.about.statement`

Job: Institutional. Make the coalition real without re-arguing the premise sitting directly beneath it.

- **PICK** — "Concerned Women Against Axe Abuse is a national coalition with a records committee, a washcloth ribbon, and one form."
  - Mechanism: defines the institution by its complete apparatus, which is also the page's own
    furniture (Findings, Tie One On, Form CW-1). A *national coalition* whose total machinery is a
    committee, a ribbon, and one form is funny without a joke, and it makes no claim the reader is
    asked to agree with.
- (a) "Concerned Women Against Axe Abuse is a national coalition of women who each have one specific man in mind."
  - Mechanism: defines it by membership, not apparatus. Sets up `close.traces` and the pledge
    referral. Risk: it is a better second line than a first one.
- (b) "Concerned Women Against Axe Abuse is a national coalition. We were not organized around a cause. We were organized around a smell."
  - Mechanism: founding fact, physical. Risk: not-X-but-Y is the balanced antithesis all three
    readers have punished elsewhere. Tested because if it survives, it is the strongest line here.

## 2 · `home.about.body[0]`, second sentence

Current DRAFT: "We document what happens when fragrance is asked to do a shower's job, and we give
people a practical way to act."

- **PICK** — **cut it.** `body[0]` becomes the APPROVED subline alone: "Routine washing is a reasonable collective expectation."
  - Mechanism: the cut *is* the fix. Both halves are covered two blocks later by DOCUMENT and
    ORGANIZE; keeping them here is the throat-clearing Priya named. The paragraph gets shorter and
    the premise gets stated exactly once.
- (a) "We document what happens when fragrance is asked to do a shower's job."
  - Keeps the documenting clause, drops the duplicate. Safest; still restates DOCUMENT.
- (b) "We write down what happened, to whom, and how long the ride was."
  - Procedural and physical, and it hands the elevator to the methodology note below. Risk: the
    last clause is a punchline in a paragraph that should not have one.

## 3 · `home.dao.run[]`

Labels are fixed. Bodies are the lever.

**PICK — set A (one operational fact each, deliberately unequal lengths):**

| Label | Body |
|---|---|
| Document | "Every finding is numbered, dated, and entered by the Field Data Committee. The committee is four women and a binder." |
| Advocate | "We state the expectation in public and put a number on it. So far no one has argued with it out loud." |
| Organize | "Chapters take the referrals. Most of them come from the woman who shares the bed." |

Mechanism: each clause names apparatus the site actually has, then one dumb physical detail. The
Organize clause is the only place on the home that says where a referral comes from, which is the
coalition's real mechanic and the least generic sentence in the block.

**(a) — set B, Advocate raised.** Document and Organize as in set A; Advocate becomes:

> "We say it in public and we keep saying it. Wash. We are done phrasing it gently."

This is the single test of Priya's standing note — "the site never once gets mad. Dryness is the
whole register; one line that breaks it would tell me the writer has range instead of a setting."
Advocacy is the one clause where an institution is licensed to raise its voice. Proposal only; CWAAA
performs no horror and has never shouted, so this ships only if the readers say the range is worth
the break.

**(b) — set C, driest:**

| Label | Body |
|---|---|
| Document | "Findings are numbered and dated. Nothing is entered that a member did not sit through." |
| Advocate | "Someone has to say it out loud, with her name attached." |
| Organize | "A referral is a name, an address, and one woman prepared to knock." |

Mechanism: pure procedure, no punchlines. Risk: "prepared to knock" edges toward the menace Priya
barred from CWAAA, though `/findings` now ratifies "answered the door clean."

Three traces, not two ("a few human chapter traces", design.md §5 item 6). The renderer maps the
array, so count is copy's decision. No invented place, chapter, or contact appears.

**PICK:**
