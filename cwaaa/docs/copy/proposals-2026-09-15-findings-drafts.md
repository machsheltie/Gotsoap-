## 1. `home.finding.support` (DRAFT)

**Current:** "Body spray may alter the immediate air. It does not remove sweat, oil, dirt, or the
consequences of avoiding soap and water."

**Diagnosis:** "may alter" is a permission cough; "the consequences of avoiding soap and water" explains
the finding instead of being it, and takes eleven words to point at him without looking.

- **A — PICK:** "Body spray treats the air. Sweat, oil, and the week stay on the person."
  *Mechanism:* the committee grants the product its one true claim, then files what it does not do;
  "the week" is the specificity dagger without inventing a number, and "the person" keeps him
  unnamed and human.
- **B:** "Body spray changes what a room smells like. It does not change what is in the room."
  *Mechanism:* parallel grammar with a turn on the last clause; "what is in the room" is him,
  described by location rather than by verdict.
- **C:** "Body spray addresses the air. The Coalition's concern is the man standing in it."
  *Mechanism:* the misdirection is the joke: the product solved a problem nobody filed.

## 2. `home.finding.method.body` (DRAFT)

**Current:** "Observations were collected at chapter tables, gym benches, and one very long elevator
ride. The committee declines to name the elevator."

**Diagnosis:** "very" fails Stepford check 3. The closer is a refusal joke, and the APPROVED
`findings.method.body` on the next route is also a refusal joke ("Please do not request it.") — same
template, second exposure. The elevator is good and stays; the second sentence needs a different
engine.

- **A — PICK:** "Observations were collected at chapter tables, on gym benches, and in one elevator.
  No member was required to stay for the full ride."
  *Mechanism:* a humane procedural concession does the describing; nothing about the air is stated,
  and the reader supplies it.
- **B:** "Observations were collected at chapter tables, on gym benches, and in elevators. No
  observation lasted longer than the ride."
  *Mechanism:* the study's limit is the observer's endurance, filed as a methodology constraint.
- **C:** "Observations were collected at chapter tables, on gym benches, and in one elevator. The
  elevator is not identified in this record."
  *Mechanism:* the refusal, kept but flattened to records language; offered only if the owner wants
  the refusal shape preserved on the home. Weakest of the three for the reason in the diagnosis.

## 4. `findings.register.note` (DRAFT)

**Current:** "Findings are entered by the Field Data Committee and remain current until superseded."

**Diagnosis:** the Field Data Committee is already credited twice on this route (lead record, method
note); a third credit is throat-clearing. "remain" is an Office-adjacent verb this site should not
get in the habit of. The line states a rule and stops; the rule should indict.

- **A — PICK:** "A finding stays current until a later finding replaces it. None has."
  *Mechanism:* the last two words are a finding about the findings; the dropped "since 1983" engine
  returns as structure instead of a fabricated date.
- **B:** "Findings stay current until superseded. Supersession requires a change in the underlying
  behavior."
  *Mechanism:* the register explains its own procedure and the procedure places the burden on him.
- **C:** "Findings stay current until superseded. The committee has not yet had the occasion."
  *Mechanism:* the polite passive; the occasion is a man showering, and it has not arrived.

## 5. `findings.register.heading` and columns

**Current:** heading "Entered findings" · columns Finding / Figure / "Entered as".

- **Heading — keep.** Structural chrome under style-lock §4, all three conditions hold: it is
  wayfinding, the register format supplies the context, and any voiced heading would promise more
  than a table delivers.
- **Finding, Figure — keep.**
- **"Entered as" → "As entered" — PICK, narrow margin.** *Mechanism:* "Entered as" implies an alias
  or a category; "As entered" is the archival term for verbatim text, which is exactly what the
  byte-carried APPROVED rows are. If the owner reads this as a coin flip, keep "Entered as" — the
  gain is precision, not a joke.

## 6. `findings.next.statement` (DRAFT)

**Current:** "The record shows the problem. The stories show it can change."

**Diagnosis:** tidy two-beat parallel that resolves into reassurance ("it can change") — the exact
flinch Priya named. Also "the stories" is not the program's name.

- **A — PICK:** "A figure is a man the committee could count. A Recovery Story is one it no longer
  needs to."
  *Mechanism:* the count drops when he showers; humane by construction (he is a participant who
  left the column), and the exit to the next route is the sentence itself.
- **B:** "The register records the figures. The Recovery Stories record the men who left them."
  *Mechanism:* parallel with a turn on "left them"; plainer than A, safer, less nerve.
- **C:** "Every figure above was a man before it was a figure. The Recovery Stories are where some
  of them went next."
  *Mechanism:* re-humanizes the table; "some of them" is honest without a hug.
