Five words, five different axes — **prompting, contest, proof, time, condition.** No two convict the
same thing. None is *unprompted*. If any single one of these is rejected, the replacement has to come
off an axis none of the others occupies, or the table stops working.

**`again` — RATIFIED 2026-09-15. Closed.** I flagged it as an adverb of frequency rather than of
manner, sitting slightly outside the shape of the other four. The flag was taken and overruled in my
favour: the register's rule is *one adverb that implies the prior state without describing it* and
never required a manner adverb. `again` is the only word in the set carrying a before *and* a
before-the-before, which is Gary's entire record in five letters, and the flatness of the field is
what keeps it from being sentimental.

**The fallback is struck.** `Lathers deliberately` was my alternate for that slot and it is no longer
on the table. It is recorded here as withdrawn so that a later reader does not find it and treat the
slot as still open. `Lathers again` is the ratified value for RC-047.
