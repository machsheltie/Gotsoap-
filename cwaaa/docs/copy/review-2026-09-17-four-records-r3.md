**Vivian, 2026-09-17. Diagnostic only.** No runtime file, record string, canon document, or PRD was
touched. Three blind reads (Maya, Dylan, Priya) were run on the revision-2 copy as a visitor meets
it — persona and copy only, no rationale, no prior reports, no rulings. Their full reports are

Fact / inference / recommendation are marked where it matters.

## 1 · Project understanding

**Got Soap?** converts through desire. Cleanliness is the price of admission to the man the visitor
already imagines being. The man is a convert-in-waiting; the woman is a witness and recruiter. It
does not lecture, diagnose, or humiliate.

**CWAAA** files. Its proposition is THE BAR IS SOAP and its engine is competent civic procedure aimed
at an absurdly low baseline — the joke is that somebody finally wrote it down. It validates a
reasonable expectation, gives witnesses language and a practical action, documents consequence,
supports change, follows up, publishes. It has no power to inspect, find, or order, and it never
winks.

**Recovery Stories** are Convert + Character surfaces inside CWAAA's register. Each one has to do
four things at once: show a believable before-state and its cost to someone else; show change that is
earned and worth wanting; keep the man recognizably himself; and read as a different person passing
through the same calm institution. The satirical target is the chosen rationalization (fragrance as a
substitute, the theory that made not-washing feel reasonable), never the man's identity, region,
class, age, or a medical barrier. The ethical line is that fictional men may be ridiculed for their
logic and fictional witnesses may be disgusted, but the institution does not exhibit a body, name a
real person, or claim regulatory authority.

The four records also have a structural job the reader never sees: they are the run of ordinary
files the archive boundary fails against. They must feel routine so Billy Bob feels like a floor
giving way.

## 3 · Independent findings

Ordered by priority as briefed. Each carries: where, evidence, type, what happens now, why it fails,
direction, and whether it should become a rule.

### 3.1 Causality, knowledge, privacy, institutional logic

**F1 — Marcus has two referral paths and no contact row. Fact.**
Index (shipped): *"A coworker forwarded me the Crisis page — anonymously."* Record referral:
*"Reporting party — Colleague. Anonymity requested and granted."* Testimony: *"a page turns up in my
inbox from a sender I still cannot identify."* Chronology opens *"Participant response — Requested the
supporting figure and its source."* Response to what? The outreach row was cut in revision 2.
*What happens now:* the reader assembles that the colleague sent Marcus the page directly AND filed a
statement with CWAAA, and that CWAAA then contacted Marcus somehow, off-page. Priya: "two different
referral paths that don't quite meet." *Why it fails:* the whole comic mechanism of this record is
"a conversion on paper between two people who never met," and the paper trail is the one thing the
record does not show. *Direction:* pick one path and let the chronology carry it. The stronger
choice is Marcus-initiated: he received the page anonymously, wrote to CWAAA himself to dispute the
figure, and CWAAA already had the colleague's referral on file. That turns the missing row into
corroboration, matches *"Requested the supporting figure and its source"* as a first act, and is more
Marcus than being contacted — he goes looking for the citation to beat. *Rule:* yes. Every chronology
must show how contact between CWAAA and the participant began. "Referral received" was cut as filler;
"how we reached him" is not filler.

**F2 — Marcus's colleague is identifiable to the one reader who matters. Inference.**
*"I sit two desks from him."* *"We had a seating change in the spring and I watched two people
volunteer for the worst desk."* Published with the participant's consent means Marcus reads this. Two
desks, one spring, a specific seating change: he knows who she is by the second sentence. *What
happens now:* CWAAA's *"Anonymity requested and granted"* is a promise the published statement breaks.
The colleague's own reason for anonymity — *"he would have been kind about it, and I couldn't have
taken that"* — is defeated by the record. Second-order: Maya read the colleague as a woman and then met
*"A woman at work put her hand on my arm."* Nothing prevents the reader from closing that loop, and
if they do, the colleague becomes his reward, which is the exact opposite of what she said. *Why it
fails:* the institution looks like it does not understand its own anonymity grant, and the record's
best paragraph becomes a liability. *Direction:* keep the elevator, which is universal; remove the
locating details (*two desks*, *the spring*, the specific seating change) or move them into CWAAA's
referral as a paraphrase without positions. Make the arm-touch woman unambiguously not the colleague
if the ending survives (see F9). *Rule:* yes. Anonymous reporting-party statements cannot contain
details that identify the party to the participant.

**F3 — Kaelthas: CWAAA arrived after the work was done and the record does not say so. Fact.**
Index (shipped): he showered, opened the window, and washed the hoodie because *"she was real now,
and driving."* Record chronology: *"Materials provided — Standard cleansing guidance. Ventilation
discussed."* then *"Revised routine reported complete before the stated interval elapsed."* *What
happens now:* the record implies CWAAA's guidance produced the cleanup. The index says a woman in a
car did. Priya "noticed the hand." Dylan's one uneasy moment about the organization is this record:
"a real snitch form." *Why it fails:* CWAAA is claiming a result it did not cause, and a coalition
that documents honestly is the entire brand. *Direction:* let the record admit it. A chronology row
on the order of *"Participant reported cleansing already in progress at time of contact"* is funnier,
truer, and gives the collection its one file where the institution was late. *"Ventilation discussed"*
survives as CWAAA's actual contribution — the window — and *"Lathers regardless"* is then earned by
the follow-up, not the intake. *Rule:* yes. The chronology records what CWAAA did, not what happened
while CWAAA existed.

**F4 — Kaelthas: what the viewer could know. Inference, minor.**
*"Reported cleansing frequency — Tied to in-person plans"* and *"Reported ventilation — None"* are
labeled *Reported*, which can mean participant-reported at intake. The viewer's statement claims only
the on-stream question and the four-hour drive, both plausibly said on stream. This holds. What does
not quite hold is CWAAA identifying and reaching a streamer within eleven minutes from a viewer's
form. It is accepted as a joke by two readers and I would leave it, but the outreach row should say
what channel was used (his broadcast chat, his handle) so the speed reads as the boy being online,
not the coalition being omniscient.

**F5 — Chad: "One variable" is false and the record lets him have it. Fact.**
He lists four changes: soap, post-gym shower, washed sheets, and by implication the apartment. Then:
*"Same face, same material, same apartment. One variable."* The chronology and close never correct
the count. *What happens now:* readers who like Chad take it as his dialect; readers who don't take it
as the record's endorsement. *Why it fails:* CWAAA is supposed to understand more than the participant.
Here it understands less, or is silent. *Direction:* keep the line as his error — it is true to a man
who only accepts results he can frame as a controlled test — and let the institution quietly know
better in one row: e.g. a chronology entry that lists what was actually revised, in CWAAA's flat
plural. The joke improves: he says one variable, the file says four. *Rule:* no; specific to Chad.

**F6 — Chad's referral is coherent. Fact, for the record.**
The Sniff Test is *"Field Assessment CW-7 · Administered by CWAAA field assessors. Results forwarded
automatically"* on the Got Soap? side. A result already sits with CWAAA; he asks for a review. The
self-referral, the voided field, and *"Asked whether the assessment could be retaken"* are the most
legible institutional chain of the four. Priya: "the cleanest logic in the set." The open question
from the proposal (may CWAAA name CW-7 on its own records) is answered by the runtime: it already
administers it.

**F7 — Gary: the index and the daughter's statement assign her opposite jobs. Fact.**
Index (shipped, owner-approved): *"Three dates this month. She screens them now."* Statement:
*"I want it on the record that I am not his mother. I filed this so it would be somebody's job other
than mine."* *What happens now:* on the index she has taken over vetting his dates; one click later
she says she filed precisely to stop being that person. This is not a voice difference; it is a
factual contradiction about what she does. *Why it fails:* her statement is the clearest articulation
in the archive of why CWAAA exists, and the index undercuts it before the reader arrives. *Direction —
three routes:* (a) reopen the shipped pull-quote (cost: owner-approved copy, homepage-adjacent
stability); (b) drop *"somebody's job other than mine"* from her closer while keeping the ratified
*"I am not his mother"* (cost: the best sentence about women's labor in the set); (c) leave both and
let Gary's testimony own the contradiction — she said it was not her job and screens them anyway,
which is what daughters do, and is the most human thing in the file. Route (c) requires changing
Gary's ending, which the ending-template finding (F10) wants anyway. **This requires an owner
decision** because two of the three routes touch ratified or shipped strings.

**F8 — Gary: decline versus depression. Inference; watch item, not a defect.**
*"Sometime after the divorce."* *"He got quiet, which was worse."* *"Some days I'd sit down when I got
in and that was the day."* Many readers will read this as depression. The satire holds because the
target is *"four theories and all of them were about her"* — the rationalization, not the decline —
and because he acknowledges immediately. It would break if any revision made CWAAA brisker with him
or made his year a joke. *"She never used to ask me anything"* implies a distance older than the
divorce that nothing in the record supports; either the daughter's statement earns it (one clause
about the year she stopped calling) or the line should not claim more history than the file has.

### 3.2 Character differentiation

**F9 — One cognitive architecture, four vocabularies. Fact, confirmed by all three readers.**
Every testimony runs: state the old private theory → explain why it felt logical → name the missed
social signal → list what changed, in fragments → report the result → a woman's gesture. Chad:
*"That's it. That's the entire list."* Marcus: *"So: soap."* Gary: *"The program is not complicated.
Shower every day. Clean shirt…"* Kaelthas: *"Shower. Window. … Sheets."* Each man also has exactly one
aphorism and exactly one exact callback. Nobody misremembers, contradicts his referral block, or
emphasizes the wrong detail. *Why it fails:* four men with total retrospective insight into their own
psychology is one writer. The differentiation the readers credited — Gary's tired clauses, Marcus's
precision, Kaelthas's gamer-adjacent register — is real, and it is vocabulary. Their minds are the
same mind. *Direction:* give each man something he still gets wrong after recovering, and let the
paragraph structures differ in kind, not just diction (§4 has the per-man proposals). *Rule:* yes, as
a test rather than a template — before shipping any testimony, name what the speaker still
misunderstands. If the answer is "nothing," the speaker is Vivian.

**F10 — One sentence in three mouths. Fact.**
Colleague: *"What people do instead is stop standing near you, and you can go years without anyone
telling you that's what's happening."* Marcus: *"what they do instead of telling you is they stop
standing close, and you do not notice a thing that stops happening."* Kaelthas: *"I hadn't known I was
braced for a face until it stopped happening."* All three readers caught it. This is the thesis of the
whole site — the invisible negative feedback loop — and it is a good thesis, which is why it has
leaked. *Direction:* it belongs to one speaker. The colleague owns it; she is the person it happened
around. Marcus should arrive at the same fact by a road that is his (the receipt is already that
road; the sentence before it is the leak). Kaelthas's closing clause needs a different verb of
recognition. *Rule:* yes. A thesis sentence is a scarce payoff and has one address.

**F11 — Three of four address the file. Fact.**
*"which I would like on the record"* (Chad), *"I want it on the record"* (daughter), *"I want that in
the file"* (Kaelthas). One is a device; three is the house style. The daughter's is the one that means
something — she is a reluctant filer insisting on her own limits. Chad's and Kaelthas's are charm.
*Direction:* cut two, keep hers.

### 3.3 Thematic and ethical consequences

**F12 — Every recovery is proved by a woman's body or attention. Fact.**
Toothbrush; hand on arm; dates and a daughter's question; return visits and no face. Maya: "I noticed
I was the outcome column." Priya: "a woman who is a person instead of a reward." Dylan did not notice,
because for him it is the offer. *What happens now:* the collection's unintended proposition is that
women file, women carry the cost, the man washes, and a woman rewards him. This is Got Soap?'s
conversion engine ported wholesale into CWAAA, whose audience is the woman reading. *Why it fails:*
CWAAA's job is collective relief and a wider account of what recovery buys — a room you can stand in,
a desk nobody avoids, a shirt from a drawer. The romantic proof is legitimate on one or two records; on
four it is an ideology. *Direction:* at least two of the four endings should prove change with
something that is not a woman's approval. Gary's daughter already gives one route (F7c). Kaelthas's
*"on days when nobody is coming over"* is the honest proof and it is currently his penultimate beat,
demoted under the face. *Rule:* yes, as a collection rule — not every ordinary record may close on a
romantic or sexual reward.

**F13 — Kaelthas's woman is a raid drop. Fact, with a fork.**
Index: *"Top of the server, miles out of my league… Best raid of my life."* Record: *"Top of the
server, and she drove to me. I want that in the file."* Maya abandoned the page here. Dylan wants to be
him. Both are correct readings of the same line. *What happens now:* the line is true to a 22-year-old
streamer and it is the single point at which a woman reader leaves CWAAA's site. *Direction:* the
line is his; the problem is that the record never lets her be anything else. One concrete thing she
did or said that was not about him — she opened the window herself, she brought something, she named
the smell — would make her a person without making him less himself. The index quote is shipped;
the record can correct for it.

**F14 — Every witness pre-forgives. Fact.**
*"he is good at his job and that this is the only thing."* *"he was allowed a bad year."* *"He's
funny, and he's decent to people in his chat."* Same slot, same function. The colleague's is the only
one that is *hers* — she is explaining why she needed anonymity. The daughter's grace-year is
plausible. The viewer's is a character reference the record needed, not one the viewer did. *Why it
fails:* CWAAA does not require its witnesses to protect the participant, and a witness who is enjoying
this would be truer to the coalition's origin as a grievance log. *Direction:* keep the colleague's;
let the viewer be a fan who is delighted to finally have a form (which is what filing "while he was
still live" already implies and the statement then apologizes for). *Rule:* not as a rule; as a
collection check — witnesses should differ in stance, not just in relationship label.

**F15 — Conversion is instantaneous, four for four. Fact.**
*"Position withdrawn same day."* *"Routine revised same day."* *"Revised routine reported complete
before the stated interval elapsed."* Chad: 30 days, maintained. Maya: "the guy folded 'same day.' I've
never met that man. That's the one place it felt like an ad." Priya: "a dispute that stuck." *Why it
fails:* Billy Bob provides failure past the boundary, but the ordinary visitor never meets him, so
the public set says the product works instantly every time. A closed case need not be a frictionless
one. *Direction:* one follow-up row on one record showing a lapse and a restart — Kaelthas is the
natural site: a gap when no visit was scheduled, then held, and *regardless* becomes a thing he
earned rather than a thing the adverb asserts. Case still closes. **Owner decision** because it
changes the shape of the routine set.

**F16 — Chad's twenty-two women. Inference.**
The opener registers the cost — *"I did not keep that one"* is the hardest line in his file. The

and the ending awards him a full victory: *"That is the most romantic thing I have ever done and she

### 3.5 Voice and sentence-level

Writer-grade lines confirmed by two or more readers independently: *"That's measurable"*, *"One
variable"*, *"I had a receipt"*, *"It just isn't load-bearing"*, *"catastrophic waste of resources"*,
*"I'd reasoned that out and I was pleased with it"*, *"before I abandon it"*, *"Vanity got me in the
door. Soap kept me there"* (shipped). The pattern: each man is permitted exactly one polished
aphorism, and it is always in Vivian's cadence, not his.

Unusually human, protect: *"I did not keep that one"*; *"I decided that was me being a lot. Intense."*;
*"Nobody said why. Nobody ever says why."*; *"That is a horrible thing to be good at"*; *"he would
have been kind about it, and I couldn't have taken that"*; *"I read that sentence four times looking
for where it was wrong"*; *"I sat in my car doing the math on what that meant for some woman I have
never met"*; *"He got quiet, which was worse"*; *"is it that bad"*; *"Some days I'd sit down when I got
in and that was the day"*; *"I wasn't filthy. Nobody was checking"*; *"four theories and all of them
were about her"*; *"getting dressed up for nobody"*; *"came out a different color, which I am still
processing"*; *"Two bags of garbage I'm not going to itemize"*; every outcome adverb; *"Asked whether
the assessment could be retaken"*; *"Score unchanged"*; *"'Warm.'"*.

## 5 · AI-tell audit

**Phrase-level clichés.** Few. *load-bearing* (Priya: 2026 copy-Twitter), *catastrophic waste of
resources*, *measurable*. Not the main problem.

**Sentence-level over-polish.** One aphorism per man, always balanced, always Vivian's rhythm:
*"Same face, same material, same apartment. One variable."* / *"I wasn't reading the room for a
signal. I had a receipt."* / *"Four hours is long enough to fix a room and short enough that you
can't sit down and think about it."* Each is good. The mechanism is that every speaker is allowed
one, in roughly the same position, and no speaker is allowed to be bad at sentences.

**Metaphor over-coordination.** Marcus: *ninety-dollar bottle → arithmetic → math → reapplying →
receipt → figure and its source → garnish → load-bearing*. Eight terms in one metaphor family across
index, referral, testimony, and chronology. A real man has one metaphor he overuses, not a
coordinated system. Chad's *kept the number / did not keep that one / measurable / one variable* is
the same thing at smaller scale and works better because it is his actual personality (he scores
things) rather than a writer's theme.

**Structural symmetry.** F9: identical paragraph functions in identical order; the fragment-list of
changes in every record; the quoted-line-as-detonation in every referral block; the character
reference opening every witness statement; the final-position stinger on every index quote.

**Emotional-template repetition.** F12: the withheld small grace, delivered by a woman, in the last
line, four times. Confirmed three rounds running.

**Fake specificity.** Mostly earned — the spray count, the chair, the drawer, eleven minutes, the
hoodie's color. Two exceptions: *"two desks"* and *"the spring"* are specific in a way that costs the
colleague her anonymity rather than buying credibility; *"eleven hundred people"* is a number the
viewer would know and is fine.

**Over-explained psychology.** Every man narrates his own self-deception with full architecture:
*"I decided that was me being a lot"*; *"I wasn't reading the room for a signal"*; *"I had about four
theories"*; *"I'd reasoned that out and I was pleased with it."* Individually each is a good line.
Collectively it means nobody in the archive is still wrong about himself, which is not how people who
just changed a habit talk. Priya's formulation: "Nobody rambles and nobody is wrong."

**Unusually human — protect.** Listed in §3.5. The pattern in that list is instructive: the human
lines are short, declarative, and slightly under-explained. The tells are the lines that explain.

## 6 · Repair options

### Conservative — fix logic and the worst repetition, keep every ratified string

Restore a contact row to Marcus (F1) and strip the colleague's locating details (F2). Add one
"already in progress" row to Kaelthas (F3). Cut two of the three "on the record"s (F11). Give the

instances to two; nothing ratified moves. One short pass.

### Recommended — rebuild the four testimonies around four minds and four proofs

Everything in Conservative, plus: each man gets a residual misunderstanding (§4) and a paragraph
shape that is his — Chad counts and ranks; Marcus argues with the figure and never describes a smell;
Gary's sentences run on and trail off, and one goes nowhere; Kaelthas inventories objects and gets the
order wrong. The fragment-list paragraph survives on one record only. Four categorically different
endings: Chad spoils his (the score); Marcus's is spatial (the desk, the elevator); Gary's is
maintenance (the drawer), or carries the daughter's contradiction if F7(c) is chosen; Kaelthas's is a
day with no one coming. Marcus's Finding is dramatized — he quotes the number and tries to beat it —

*Cost:* four testimonies substantially rewritten; the *"I'm hotter now"* and Chad's opener survive but
their surroundings change; a blind-read round is required before ship; the routine set is no longer
uniformly instantaneous if F15 is taken.
*Benefit:* directly answers the finding that has converged three rounds running; keeps the forms,
chronologies, and adverbs — the part every reader called the product — untouched; gives CWAAA a wider
account of recovery than a woman's touch; keeps the closes identical and lets them read as the
archive pattern instead of as the fourth copy of a template.

### Bold — stop making every man deliver a monologue

Represent testimony differently per record, inside forms CWAAA already owns. Chad's testimony as a
disputed-assessment response: numbered, argumentative, addressed to the score. Marcus's as
correspondence — his letter contesting Finding 26-01 and CWAAA's one-paragraph reply, so the
"conversion on paper" happens on paper. Gary's as a follow-up call transcribed by a caseworker, with
her paraphrase where he trailed off. Kaelthas's as an intake completed in a hurry — short answers to
CWAAA's questions, one of them wrong, timestamped inside the four hours. The reporting-party
statements stay as prose because the witnesses are the people who chose to write.
*Cost:* a template change on the route (four testimony renderers instead of one), a design pass, and
the loss of the "testimony in Modest, widest quietest field" uniformity the surface brief specifies.
Highest risk of over-producing bureaucracy for its own sake. Brayden and Billy Bob keep the current
form, which would then be two of six rather than the rule.
*Benefit:* the one-writer problem becomes structurally impossible because the forms do the
differentiating; the institutional-logic findings resolve by construction (the letter *is* the contact
chain; the hurried intake *is* the eleven minutes); Priya's "the form is the product" becomes the
design. This is the only option under which a fifth record would not be skimmed.

**My recommendation is Recommended.** Bold is the better idea and the wrong time: the route's job
right now is a run of ordinary files for the boundary to fail against, and four differently-shaped
testimonies make the ordinary files extraordinary. Recommended fixes the men without changing what
a record looks like.
