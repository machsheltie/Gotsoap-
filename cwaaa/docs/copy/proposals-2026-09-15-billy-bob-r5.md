# What changed from R4

**Removed, not replaced.** Every cut below leaves space rather than substitute material.

- The non-shift washing routine, in all forms. He has no bounded workday exception. The only
  surviving reference to his days off is his own refusal of them: *"What am I washing for on a
  Sunday? Monday's coming."*
- The invented coworker validation ("Every man I work beside understands that"). It gave his
  position a constituency and made the refusal communal rather than his own.
- Two polished industrial analogies from the testimony (the chain, the curtain, and in R3 a pump and
  a belt). His proposition carries itself; a stack of them was Vivian showing range in his mouth.
- The reporting party's "I've never once minded the way he smells coming in the door." She minds it,
  has always minded it, and says so without it becoming a verdict on him or the job.
- From the field note: "answered every question I put to him," "without heat," and "He is able to do
  this." The note now runs comprehension → rejection → exhaustion and certifies nothing about his
  temperament or competence.
