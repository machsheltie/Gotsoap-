## 2 · What CWAAA is, in one paragraph

Concerned Women Against Axe Abuse, est. 2024. A fictional national advocacy coalition whose public
proposition is **THE BAR IS SOAP** — routine washing is a reasonable collective expectation. The
comedic engine is competent civic procedure aimed at an absurdly low baseline: the joke is that
someone filed it. CWAAA documents, intervenes, supports, follows up, and publishes. It holds no
regulatory power and never claims any. The satire's target is chosen low-effort behaviour and
fragrance substitution — the man who believes body spray covers for not washing — never protected
classes and never medical barriers to hygiene. CWAAA never performs horror and never winks.

Recovery Stories are **participant advocacy files, not police evidence**. Findings prove the issue;
Recovery Stories show change. Every record runs BEFORE → INTERVENTION → EXPERIMENT → RESULT → AFTER
and must land an earned change that makes the after-state worth wanting.

## 5 · The reader process

Three personas pressure-test copy by reporting felt experience. They are **not** critics and they do
not propose fixes; they report what happened to them, in order, quoting actual lines. The rule that
makes them worth anything is that **they read blind** — persona and copy only, never the brief, the
voice bible, or any statement of intent, and they are explicitly barred from searching the repo. A
reader told what to feel reports the feeling it was told to have.

They were given the copy as a reader meets it, with every trace of authorial rationale stripped.

- **Maya, 34.** Project manager, single three years, on the apps, funny and deeply over it. Arrives
  from a friend: *"SCREAMING. this is every man on hinge."* Tests the laugh of recognition,
  shareability, and punching down.
- **Dylan, 29.** IT support. Showers "usually," skips work-from-home days, proud of the beard, body
  spray as showering-but-faster, calls himself low maintenance and means it well. A date went well
  last month and she never texted back. Arrives from a friend: *"lol bro take this quiz."* Tests
  conversion and the aspiration-versus-shame line. He is the only reader who can say whether a mean
  line is working or losing the sale.
- **Priya, 41.** Creative director, irreverent DTC lane, sees ~50 portfolios a month, 45-second
  attention budget. Hunts for a point of view and can smell AI-assisted work at forty paces. Tests
  AI tells, nerve, and whether this gets the author hired.

**Interpretation rule.** Where readers converge independently, treat it as close to fact. Where they
disagree, that is a strategic fork about which surface serves which audience — surface it, do not
average it. Reports are evidence, not orders: you may argue with a reading if you have reasons, but
you may not wave one away or assert a reaction the evidence contradicts.
