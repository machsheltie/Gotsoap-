# Got Soap? shared world bible

## How to read canon

Every consequential claim uses one of four statuses: **Objective canon**, **Public claim**,
**Intentionally unresolved**, or **Historical/inherited copy**. Intentional uncertainty is not
missing documentation.

## Three authors and their powers

- Got Soap? campaigns through erotic aspiration, not shame.
- CWAAA advocates, files, and organizes as a credible nonprofit.
- The Office of Lather Compliance regulates through polite, complete error infrastructure.

## Objective canon

- Got Soap?, CWAAA, and the Office are distinct fictional entities.
- The Office is a government agency whose jurisdiction is deliberately unspecified.
- The Office has existed since 1961.
- CWAAA was established in 2024.
- CWAAA began with a book club.
- CWAAA has two million concerned women and chapters in all fifty states.
- CWAAA and the Office have a real institutional relationship.
- CWAAA and the Office are legally separate in private canon.
- CWAAA links to the Office but does not publicly define the relationship.
- Audience-facing artifacts never prove how their relationship operates.
- The Office pen is a real artifact within the world and carries the unsettling 1961 continuity.

## Public claims

- Got Soap? invites people to join a movement for clean-man energy.
- CWAAA presents itself as a competent advocacy nonprofit with chapters in all fifty states.
- CWAAA may make sparse, neutral, procedurally ordinary references to the Office when an artifact
  requires one.
- The Office appears administratively complete, but its error domain never supplies a jurisdiction
  statement or ordinary public service.

## Intentionally unresolved

- What happened in 1961.
- Why the Office existed long before CWAAA.
- Why the public has not heard of the Office.
- How CWAAA first encountered the Office or how their current relationship began.
- Whether the Office acts through CWAAA or CWAAA provides a friendly public face for it.
- Whether a caller is transferred from CWAAA to the Office and how much CWAAA leadership knows.
- What legal jurisdiction the Office claims or what lies behind an unavailable Office resource.

## Emotional laws

- GOT SOAP? SEDUCES.
- CWAAA VALIDATES AND ORGANIZES.
- THE OFFICE ASSUMES JURISDICTION.
- Got Soap? changes behavior through erotic aspiration, not shame.
- CWAAA never deliberately manufactures fear; its excessive certainty may accidentally expose a larger system.
- The Office never performs horror; it behaves as though its actions are ordinary and complete.
- No system explains the whole world. Repeated details reward attention without becoming a puzzle game.

## Mystery discipline


The Office/CWAAA ambiguity is a controlled audience experience, not a continuity error. References
to the Office stay sparse, neutral, procedurally ordinary, unexplained, and artifact-led. The IVR is
a deliberate exception: evidence of the mystery, not permission to turn ordinary CWAAA surfaces into
compliance theater.

## Shared terminology

- **Got Soap?:** the campaign author.
- **CWAAA:** the advocacy and filing author.
- **Office:** the Office of Lather Compliance, the regulator author.
- **Lather Pledge:** a shared mechanic with controlled Got Soap? and CWAAA presentations; CWAAA owns
  the declaration, filing, and receipt, and the Office never receives or enforces it.
- **Office pen:** ordinary institutional swag whose meaning can change only through recurrence.

## Prohibited explanations

Do not explain the CWAAA/Office relationship, add a clean handoff, average their rendered authorship,
or label either as the other's partner, parent agency, operator, division, coordinator, or public-facing
layer. Do not turn repeated details into puzzle keys or use audience-facing artifacts to prove operational
separation.
