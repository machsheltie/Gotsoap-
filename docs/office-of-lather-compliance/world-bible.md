# Office of Lather Compliance world bible

## Canonical identity

The Office of Lather Compliance is a separate fictional government agency. Its jurisdiction is
**DELIBERATELY UNSPECIFIED**. It is not Got Soap?, a CWAAA program, or a conventional public-service
portal. The Office regulates through polite, complete error infrastructure.

## History

The Office has existed since **1961**. That date is objective canon; what happened in 1961 and why
the Office was established are intentionally unresolved. CWAAA was established in 2024, so its later
history must never be used to explain away the Office's earlier existence.

## What the Office believes

The Office believes containment is routine, its authority is already in effect, and the requested
resource is properly unavailable. It does not regard recognition, denial, or the instruction to
remain available as exceptional. Its certainty is administrative, not theatrical.

## Voice

The Office writes with patient precision, complete sentences, controlled repetition, and quiet
institutional certainty. It never raises its voice, reaches for a punchline, or admits that the
situation might be strange. Procedure is presented as settled fact.

## Emotional law

**THE OFFICE NEVER MANUFACTURES THE FEAR; THE VISITOR SUPPLIES THE FEAR.** Calm accuracy creates the
uncanny effect. Politeness, continuity, and refusal carry more force than menace.

## What the Office never does

- It never names a federal, state, local, or invented jurisdiction.
- Its error-only website never announces assessor dispatch or enforcement, performs horror, or uses
  surveillance boasts. This website rule does not rewrite the canonical IVR: its Field Assessors and
  “someone is already on their way” language remain protected, and what that language proves about
  Office operations is intentionally unresolved.
- It never explains its whole agency, offers useful services, or becomes an ordinary website.
- It never claims to recognize a person, household, device owner, IP address, or cross-device identity.
- It never processes the Lather Pledge or CWAAA subscriber data.
- It never resolves the protected relationship questions shared with CWAAA.

## Public surface

The public surface model is **ERROR STATES ONLY**. Every public path renders an inaccessible-resource
state. There is no ordinary homepage, navigation, agency explainer, regulations library, directory,
login, or hidden useful content. Repeated access changes the error-state composition, not access to a
site behind it.

## Recognition and privacy

Recognition is browser-local and separates distinct browser sessions from reloads. Session 1 is
First Access; a reload in that session is Refresh Denied. Session 2 is Repeat Access; reloads in that
session never advance the narrative. Session 3 and every later session remain in Continued Interest
stasis. State-dependent copy is resolved before reveal so a returning browser never flashes First
Access.

Only `sessionStorage` and `localStorage` may support the effect. There are no cookies, IP-address
records, browser fingerprints, authentication records, backend identity records, server persistence,
or cross-device stitching. If storage is unavailable, the Office renders a neutral inaccessible-
resource state without falsely claiming recognition.

## 8804-X

`8804-X` is a **STANDING CONTAINMENT REFERENCE**. It belongs to the unavailable resource or standing
administrative condition; it is not visitor-specific, not a visitor identity, and not evidence of a
personal dossier. Its recurrence communicates institutional continuity.

## Terminal identity

The terminal ID is a locally generated fictional browser identifier. It may persist only in that
browser's local record. It is not derived from network data or fingerprint inputs and must never be
presented as a verified identity for the visitor or as recognition across browsers or devices.

## Relationship knowledge

The Office and CWAAA have a real institutional relationship and are legally separate in objective
canon. CWAAA may link to the Office, but neither system publicly defines how the relationship works.
The Office does not call CWAAA its parent, partner, operator, division, coordinator, or public-facing
layer.

## What remains unexplained

The following are **INTENTIONALLY UNRESOLVED**: what happened in 1961; why the public has not heard of
the Office; what jurisdiction it claims; what lies behind the unavailable resource; how CWAAA first
encountered the Office; whether either entity acts through the other; whether callers can be
transferred between them; and how much CWAAA leadership knows. No artifact supplies these answers.

## Artifact behavior

Office artifacts feel ordinary to the institution and unsettling only through recurrence. Codes,
timestamps, restrained language, and the Office pen may accumulate meaning across surfaces, but they
never become puzzle keys or proof of a hidden organizational chart. Repetition deepens continuity;
it does not explain the mystery.
