# Claude implementation brief

Treat the linked documents as executable creative constraints, not inspiration.

Before proposing or building a surface:

1. Name its fictional author: Got Soap?, CWAAA, or the Office of Lather Compliance.
2. State the visual register and the anti-default it rejects.
3. Describe one dominant composition, one interruption, and one designed transition.
4. Check the route against the target PRD and its acceptance criteria.

Do not average the three systems into a tasteful shared design system. Shared engineering primitives
may exist beneath them, but the rendered authors must remain visibly and behaviorally distinct.

The revised visual laws are compact and binding:

- Got Soap? is glossy campaign heat; Shop is a **fashion catalogue pretending to be a store**.
- **CWAAA: A Coalition Making Its Case in Public** uses dramatic, legible public advocacy; paper is
  material only when the content is an actual paper artifact.
- **The Office page must appear under-designed**: quiet, bureaucratic, error-only, and never a
  stylized terminal or useful agency site.

CWAAA public navigation uses **Recovery Stories** and **Chapters**. Privacy, Terms, and DMCA remain
accessible; fiction disclosure waits behind the creator/About seam. For the IVR, Got Soap? owns
number/placement while the operational owner and exact handoff remain intentionally unresolved.

Intentional uncertainty is not missing documentation. Do not explain the CWAAA/Office relationship,
add a clean handoff, or average their rendered authorship. When a requirement seems ambiguous,
preserve the campaign's commitment and the institution boundary. Do not “solve” uncertainty with
centered containers, equal cards, safe gradients, generic editorial serifs, pill buttons, or a
conventional agency homepage.

Use `docs/HANDOFF.md` and the applicable target design/PRD; do not restate route specifications here.

Path-scoped design constraints for the web build also live in `.claude/rules/`.
